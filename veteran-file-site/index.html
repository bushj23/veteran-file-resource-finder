PRIVACY & ACCESSIBILITY

Built to be useful without asking for sensitive Veteran information.

We keep the resource finder simple: no VA login, claim number, Social Security number, or medical information is required to search public resources.

Privacy

The Veteran File uses Google Analytics to understand general site traffic and resource usage. Analytics may collect technical information such as device type, browser, approximate location, referral source, and pages or buttons used.

The resource finder does not require a name, VA claim number, Social Security number, health information, or VA.gov credentials. ZIP searches are used to find public resources and are not intentionally sent to Google Analytics as a custom event value.

Third-party links, including VA.gov and TikTok, have their own privacy practices.

Accessibility commitment

The Veteran File is designed with accessible labels, keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and descriptive image text. We are continuing to review the site for accessibility improvements.

If you have difficulty using any part of the site, please use the official VA links provided throughout the site while we work to improve accessibility.

This statement describes our current accessibility efforts and is not a certification of full Section 508 compliance.
