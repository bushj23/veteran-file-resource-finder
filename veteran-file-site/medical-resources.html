HOW LISTINGS WORK

Useful information without implying endorsement.

Every listing should give Veterans enough information to research a provider independently.

1

Verify credentials

Listings should identify the clinician's professional credentials, specialties, and the states where they are licensed or authorized to practice.

2

Show services clearly

Each provider can identify whether they offer nexus opinions, IMOs, DBQ evaluations, records reviews, telehealth, or other relevant services.

3

Keep VA resources separate

Independent provider listings stay visually and functionally separate from official VA facility data and VA-accredited representative resources.
